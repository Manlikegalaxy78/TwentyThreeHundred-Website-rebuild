Our story

Where we came from.

TwentyThreeHundred was originally formed to provide specialist products and services in support of major HM Government transformation programmes. Created by like-minded specialist delivery professionals from the world of P3M, we have grown into a vibrant, connected and right-sized enterprise.

Since 2018, we have helped our customers deliver major IT change initiatives; formed the specialist core of PMO, CoE and Product Operations functions; helped PPM organisations move towards becoming successful Product Organisations; and planned organisation-wide migration to Cloud Services.

Our subject matter experts work alongside a skilled team of developers who design customised, user-friendly solutions to meet unique needs. Led by senior consultants with 10 to 25 years of industry expertise, our multidisciplinary team brings together extensive knowledge from various sectors.

Our expertise

Portfolio Management, Planning, Reporting, Project Management, Product Management, Business Analysis, Business Change and Technology — all in service of HM Government clients.
