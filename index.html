About us

A vibrant SME built on Civil Service delivery experience.

TwentyThreeHundred is a vibrant SME based in Cheltenham and London. We enable PMO service providers to Government to drive down costs and drive up productivity through innovative training delivery, data analysis and reporting.

The founders have spent over 60 years working in portfolio delivery and management for Civil Service clients — experience that shapes every workshop, every piece of analysis, and every dashboard we build. Our aim is always to visualise the message, and to help the people we work with focus on what matters.
