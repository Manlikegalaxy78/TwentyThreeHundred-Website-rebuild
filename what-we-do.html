What we do

Strategic planning, analytics and optimisation for Government.

We provide support throughout the entire business operations lifecycle — covering both individual and group change initiatives as well as operational execution and growth. Our expertise is in Programme, Project and Product Portfolio delivery.

What we offer

Three core areas of expertise.

Strategic Level Planning, Risk & Decision Support

We help organisations navigate complexity at the portfolio level — from strategic risk management and investment decision support, through to portfolio and programme schedule development and governance.

Our planning professionals bring deep experience of Civil Service delivery standards and governance, ensuring plans are not just technically sound but organisationally credible.

Data Capture, Engineering & Visualisation

We help organisations turn their data into actionable management information — capturing the right data, engineering reliable pipelines and building visualisations that enable confident decision-making.

From MS Excel to Qlik Sense, AWS to MS-SQL, we meet clients where their data lives and build the tools they actually need.

Performance Improvement for Individuals, Teams & Organisations

We develop the capability of people and teams across the delivery spectrum — from individual coaching and agile skills workshops to organisational change and community development.

Our coaches and consultants are experienced practitioners, not just trainers — they bring real delivery context to every engagement.

Who we serve

Specialists in Government-facing PMO.

TwentyThreeHundred was originally formed to provide specialist products and services in support of major Government transformation programmes. That heritage is still central to everything we do.

We are a supplier to AtkinsRéalis to provide programme management office services to the PMO+, and are proud to be part of the AXIA Consortium — giving us the reach to operate across a wide range of Government frameworks and contracting vehicles.

Over eight years, we have helped customers deliver major IT change initiatives; formed the specialist core of PMO, CoE and Product Operations functions; and helped PPM organisations move closer towards becoming successful Product Organisations.
