Our story

Why TwentyThreeHundred?

Picture water streaming through a channel — it may move smoothly at times or become rough and turbulent. The speed of this flow depends on system limitations, friction, and a mathematical constant called the Reynolds number.

For fluids, when the Reynolds number exceeds 2300, movement shifts from calm and predictable to chaotic and erratic. Below 2300, the flow remains stable but slower.

TwentyThreeHundred aims to elevate constraints, reduce friction, and build the conditions where maximum value can flow freely across your organisation.

Originally formed to provide specialist products and services in support of major Government transformation programmes, TwentyThreeHundred was created by like-minded specialist delivery professionals from the world of P3M. Since 2018, we have become a vibrant, connected and right-sized enterprise.

Over eight years we've helped customers deliver major IT change initiatives; formed the specialist core of PMO, CoE and Product Operations functions; helped PPM organisations move towards becoming successful Product Organisations; and planned organisation-wide migration to Cloud Services.

The Reynolds Number
Our name is our philosophy. Below the Reynolds number, flow is laminar — ordered, predictable, efficient. Above it, chaos takes hold. We work at the threshold — removing friction, elevating constraints and enabling the maximum flow of value for our clients.
